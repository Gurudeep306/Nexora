NexoraGuru avatars: three directions

Six hand-drawn SVG samples per direction. Each direction is one art style with one light rig and one palette logic; the final set will be about 16 avatars in the chosen style. Every avatar is a full-bleed circle, shown here with the app's 2 px accent ring.

AClay

Soft 3D characters: one key light, warm skin gradients, glossy eyes, a contact shadow under the chin. Friendly and premium, like Memoji drawn as vectors.

  • Most personality per pixel
  • Skin-tone range built in
  • Gradients carry the 3D, so it holds up at 24 px

BInk

Flat editorial portraits: one ink colour for every line and all hair, flat fills, a single offset shade on each face. Calm and grown-up, in the Notion and Micah family.

  • Quietest and most "product"
  • Works like a pen illustration on paper
  • Line weight softens at 24 px
